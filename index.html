INSTRUCTIONS

Hello! This is a crime game. It is based on a concept called Caesar Cipher in cyber security.

After starting the game, you will be seeing two input boxes and three buttons. There in the input boxes, you will be asked to enter a random text (meaningful or meaningless text) by the Caesar Cipher also enter the shift amount. You will find three buttons "Encrypt" "Decrypt" and "Enter the vault". After giving the text in the input boxes, you have to try both the buttons (Encrypt, Decrypt). On clicking the buttons, you can find two numbers (one for each button) nearby the buttons. Take a note of both the numbers and enter the vault by clicking on the button "Enter the vault". There you can find the bank locker. Enter the correct number out of the two to open the vault. If you enter the wrong number, you will lose the game. You can see a back button over there which can navigate you to the previous page. But, the number you previously entered will be unfound once you enter the vault. If you click the back button, you have two options. Either you have to enter a new text and find the number or you have to enter the previous number correctly. Just type the number of the locker and click the enter button, the locker will be opened. The locker will be opened only if you give the correct number. Only then, you can win the game.

Please click "Enter the palace to start".
